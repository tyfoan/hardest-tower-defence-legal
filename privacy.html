Hardest Tower Defence

Privacy Policy

Effective 29 October 2026

Hardest Tower Defence (“the game”) is an iPhone and iPad game published on the App Store by Oleksandr Ohurtsov (“we”). Contact: myonlygamess@gmail.com.

In short

  • There is no account and no sign-up. We run no servers and collect no personal data ourselves.
  • Your progress, settings, tries and purchase records stay on your device.
  • Optional rewarded video ads come from Google AdMob, which collects the data described below.
  • Apple handles payments, subscriptions and the Game Center leaderboard.

What stays on your device

  • Campaign progress, best scores, stars and settings are saved in the game’s own folder and are removed when you delete the game.
  • The tries record — free and bought tries, the time of the next free try, the ads watched in the last 24 hours and the IDs of purchases already counted — is saved in the iOS Keychain of this device only. It stays after the game is deleted, so reinstalling neither refills free tries nor loses bought ones.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else.

Purchases and subscriptions

Packs of tries and the “Unlimited tries” subscription are sold by Apple through the App Store, under Apple’s terms and privacy policy. We never see your payment details. On your device the game reads your purchases and active subscription from the App Store to give you tries or unlimited play. You can manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, the game sends your campaign’s total score to Apple’s Game Center leaderboard “Total score”, where other players can see it next to your Game Center name. Apple’s privacy policy applies to Game Center.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

You can choose to watch a rewarded video ad to get a free try; ads appear only when you ask for one. Ads are served by Google AdMob (Google Ireland Limited in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland; Google LLC elsewhere). When the ads module runs, Google may collect:

  • your IP address, which may be used to estimate your general location;
  • device identifiers that are limited to an app or developer — the game does not ask for permission to track you, so it gives Google no access to your device’s advertising identifier (IDFA);
  • advertising data, such as the ads you have seen;
  • product interactions, such as app launches, taps and video views;
  • crash logs and performance data of the ads module.

Google uses this data to show and measure ads, prevent fraud and improve its services. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google’s Privacy Policy.

Where the law requires it — in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland — the game shows Google’s consent form before ads are requested. You can change your choice at any time in the game: Settings › Privacy choices.

Children

The game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you, so there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete on our side. Data on your device is removed by deleting the game; the tries record in the Keychain is removed when the device is erased. For the data Google collects, use the privacy choices in the game and Google’s My Ad Center. You can also write to us or to your data protection authority (in Spain, the AEPD).

Changes

When the way the game handles data changes, we will update this page and its date.
