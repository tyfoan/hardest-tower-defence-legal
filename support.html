Hardest Tower Defence

Support

Write to myonlygamess@gmail.com. Please include your device model, its iOS version and what happened.

Tries

  • Sectors 1–3 are free. From sector 4 every battle start uses a try, and a victory gives it back.
  • You have up to 3 free tries; one comes back every 8 hours.
  • Watch a rewarded ad for one more free try, up to 3 times a day.
  • Buy a pack of 5 or 20 tries, or subscribe to “Unlimited tries” for a week or a month: while the subscription is active, battles use no tries.

Restoring purchases

  • Subscription: open Get tries and tap Restore.
  • Packs of tries are used up as you play. Unused tries stay on the device, also after reinstalling the game, but they do not move to another device.

Subscriptions and refunds

  • Manage or cancel a subscription in Settings › your name › Subscriptions, or at apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple: reportaproblem.apple.com.
